# Beckenburg production rules

- Closeout means finalize the requested implementation, commit it, push it to the authorized remote branch, and verify that the remote contains the commit. A plan or local-only change is not a completed delivery. Never force-push over concurrent work.
- Preserve workflow continuity with regular, scoped commits at completed implementation, asset-generation and review milestones. Push recoverable checkpoints after reconciling concurrent work; do not defer every commit until the final closeout. Record completed checks and the next unfinished step in the level workflow notes.
- Environment work must follow the approved location concepts and measured architectural references. Complete and inspect the house before expanding the approach to the other locations.
- Geometry, navigation collision, room volumes, electrical sockets, portals, acoustic mapping, spawns, and the van dock must share authored coordinates. Upper floors require authoritative elevation and continuous stairs with floor openings.
- Mount fixtures on their actual supporting surfaces. Preserve original mesh pivots and UV materials. Furniture must leave usable circulation space; source-library quality is the minimum visual standard.
- Local compilation, level verification, and preview rendering are authorized for environment work. Record actual results and unresolved limitations; do not call an unverified level production-ready.
- Coordinate with concurrent work by fetching and reconciling changes before upload. Preserve the active van implementation and connect levels through its placement contract.
- Record every newly discovered development defect in development/quality/defects.json and DEFECT_LEDGER.md, including corner/floor/wall/roof light bleed, unsupported or intersecting models, misplaced tabletop objects, circulation and regressions caused by a fix. Preserve reproduction evidence, affected sites, status, regression check and older-map audit obligations. Follow development/quality/MAP_FITNESS_CHECKLIST.md for each map/model iteration; do not infer that unaudited older maps are fixed.
- Publish map/model development captures to the shared project gallery at every checkpoint: run python tools/build_development_gallery.py and --check, commit the actual PNGs plus generated gallery/index, push, and verify the deployed page/images. Keep runtime captures, native overviews, model inspection images and rough blockouts labeled by type. This is part of ordinary map closeout.

- After rough map blocking and before terrain acceptance, apply development/quality/DESQUAREIFY_GATE.md. Remove zone-shaped ground pads/material stamps, shape the terrain mesh and physical edges around actual play, and show the canonical van in arrival reviews. A loose rectangle with perturbed corners is not accepted. Audit older maps individually.

- Every remote map requires a placed generator and authored distribution/breaker/circuit plan under development/quality/REMOTE_SITE_POWER.md. Ghost electrical events must retain normal host-authoritative breaker trip behavior. Source-running and breaker states are separate; independent oil/fire/battery lighting stays independent.
