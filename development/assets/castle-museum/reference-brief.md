Required corrections: anatomically shaped medial-ridged cuirass; real neck/arm cutouts; overlapping waist lames; two curved articulated tassets with visible straps/rivets; layered shoulder caps; shaped elbow/knee wings and limb plates; actual visor apertures; articulated foot/hand construction. Preserve intentional separate moving plates; eliminate unsupported slabs and toy spherical joints. Review actual reimported GLB front/side/rear in clay before accepting finish, then compare texture/material and actual castle placement.

Rejected studies remain under previews/rejected-primitive-armour. The earlier generic textures, slab geometry and exporter completion are not quality acceptance.
