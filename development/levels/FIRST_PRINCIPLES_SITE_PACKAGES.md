# Per-location first-principles work packages

31 October 2026. Execution companion to [the staged rebuild plan](FIRST_PRINCIPLES_REBUILD_PLAN.md). These are proposed design obligations and inspection questions, not final architectural findings or completed implementations. All 28 base locations plus the existing annex receive S00–S11 decisions in the stage register. Existing `layouts/`, `blockouts/`, `model-requirements/`, `edge-sky-plans/` and `power-plans/` packets remain inputs; inspect them rather than replacing them with these summaries.

For every site, retain its approved identity and record reference-backed dimensions during S01–S03. The functions below express the design intent to resolve, not permission to expand the playable footprint indefinitely. Deliberately omitted/non-playable functions need a credible visible boundary or explanation.

## Domestic places and headquarters

### HOUSE — The Briar House (`house`)

- **Organizing principle:** A household with public entry/living areas, a coherent kitchen/dining relationship, private bedrooms and cellar services. Additions should explain any irregularity.
- **Rough/blockout:** Inspect the current ground-floor loop and both stair flights, bedroom privacy, bathroom access, rear garden, cellar and canonical van approach. Check the actual apertures/clearances before retaining them.
- **Reuse/refine:** Preserve suitable domestic assets, clean/worn furniture and useful authored stairs/trim. Group seating around activity, kitchen fittings around preparation, and cellar services around maintenance access.
- **Acceptance focus:** House is the method pilot. Review stair/deck sections, wall-mounted services, tabletop supports, domestic scale and readable family traces under ordinary and blackout lighting.

### FARMHOUSE — Hollowmere Farm (`farmhouse`)

- **Organizing principle:** A working farmstead: house, yard, barn and service buildings linked by domestic and agricultural routines.
- **Rough/blockout:** Separate household arrival from workyard delivery; verify barn access, hayloft stair/opening, storage, tool maintenance, drainage and routes across the yard.
- **Reuse/refine:** Retain credible house/barn assemblies, farm implements, timber and household assets. Place hay/feed/tools where the relevant activity occurs; keep tall barn structure distinct from domestic rooms.
- **Acceptance focus:** Mud/water follows terrain and traffic, machinery has access, loft guards work, and outside crossings remain readable and traversable in darkness.

### MANSION — Blackthorn Manor (`mansion`)

- **Organizing principle:** An estate with ceremonial arrival, reception rooms, private accommodation and a coherent servant/service circulation network.
- **Rough/blockout:** Evaluate the asymmetric proposed estate against the generic runtime cross-wing. Locate service access, kitchen/pantry, vertical circulation, garden frontage and public/private thresholds before ornaments.
- **Reuse/refine:** Retain fitting inherited furniture, portraits and domestic fixtures; author missing architectural bays or service fittings only after fit review. Room scale must follow function, not enlarged House boxes.
- **Acceptance focus:** A visitor can read status and use from the layout; staff routes actually connect; richly dressed rooms preserve movement and do not all become equivalent parlors.

### CASTLE — Castle Beckenburg (`castle`)

- **Organizing principle:** A historic fortified complex adapted into a working investigator headquarters, with a readable arrival/courtyard and compatible modern operations.
- **Rough/blockout:** Inspect the existing rebuild before replacement. Preserve active armory, equipment stock, mission/campaign boards, records, options station, library, lounge, tutorial/practice functions, upper routes and actual gate exit.
- **Reuse/refine:** Retain suitable masonry/timber assemblies, headquarters fixtures and accepted source models. Distinguish historic construction from later cables, stores and operational workstations.
- **Acceptance focus:** Prove the complete Castle -> case -> Castle journey, station aim/reach, physical equipment access, stairs/ramparts and exit countdown. HQ does not receive fictitious investigation ritual or hunt requirements.

## Institutional and civic places

### SCHOOL — Ravenshade School (`school`)

- **Organizing principle:** A school day: arrival/admin, classrooms, specialist teaching, library, assembly/gym, meals, student welfare and service plant.
- **Rough/blockout:** Review classroom capacity and aisle layout, gym/assembly height, stair/exit distribution, kitchen deliveries, washrooms and changing facilities. Resolve the documented upper locker-corridor child-washroom arrangement physically.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse desks, blackboards, lockers, sanitary fittings and plant where their actual dimensions fit. Provide coherent teaching positions, sightlines, coat/storage and maintained service spaces.
- **Acceptance focus:** Inspect every stall and entrance, classroom egress, gym support facilities and service basement. Empty architecture must read as a school before noticeboards or abandoned papers are added.

### PRISON — Beckenburg Penitentiary (`prison`)

- **Organizing principle:** Controlled movement through sally port/intake, supervision, secured cell wings, exercise, medical/mess and staff/service areas.
- **Rough/blockout:** Use the existing compound proposal as an input; replace generic runtime rooms only after building real cells, gallery voids/guards, stairs, gate openings and a credible access hierarchy.
- **Reuse/refine:** Search existing bunks, sanitation, metal doors/bars, benches and service assets. Build missing secure cell/gallery kits explicitly; never approximate individual cells with renamed open rectangles.
- **Acceptance focus:** Cell doors, narrow approaches, supervisory sightlines, yard links and emergency/extraction routes work for lawful hunter and ghost movement. Gate state must not trap a case irrecoverably.

### ASYLUM — St. Dymphna Hospital (`asylum`)

- **Organizing principle:** An institution organized around patient wards, observation, treatment, sanitation and staff support, with readable circulation and service separation.
- **Rough/blockout:** Evaluate the staggered-ward proposal; distinguish admission, ward clusters, treatment, washrooms, linen/food service, staff rooms and plant. Resolve privacy, daylight, courtyard relationships and stairs.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable beds, storage, sanitary and medical/service assets without turning every space into an identical bed room. Medical props require actual task surfaces and operating space.
- **Acceptance focus:** The care/work pattern remains intelligible beneath abandonment. Ward doors, bathrooms, bedside movement, vertical routes and hospital-scale volumes pass individual review.

### ACADEMY — St. Alban Academy (`academy`)

- **Organizing principle:** A residential teaching institution using its proposed cloister/quadrangle structure to connect study, communal life, residence and services.
- **Rough/blockout:** Distinguish it from Ravenshade through a coherent courtyard, teaching/study sequences, residential privacy and kitchen/dining/service access. Review weather-covered links and upper gallery support.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse appropriate desks, bookshelves, dining furniture, beds and fixtures; author characteristic architectural rhythm where the source kit does not fit.
- **Acceptance focus:** Courtyard crossings, cloisters, dormitory services and stairs have purpose and actual support. Repeated bays serve construction and use rather than disguising another generic cross-wing.

### COLLEGE — Beckenburg College (`college`)

- **Organizing principle:** A small campus of distinct academic buildings with meaningful outdoor connections, shared library/bridge and services.
- **Rough/blockout:** Review the proposed separated buildings, entrances, covered/exposed links, library bridge support, service connections, building heights and nighttime wayfinding.
- **Reuse/refine:** Share teaching/library assets where appropriate, but give departments and communal spaces different arrangements and equipment tied to their brief.
- **Acceptance focus:** Building transitions, bridge approaches/undersides, outdoor pursuit loops and multi-floor ownership agree physically and acoustically. Campus identity must survive removal of signs.

### CHURCH — St. Brigid Parish (`church`)

- **Organizing principle:** A worship building with nave/chancel, associated parish/service rooms and credible vertical access to choir, belfry and crypt.
- **Rough/blockout:** Audit existing bespoke geometry, ceremonial entrance, seating/aisles, sacristy/office service routes, loft support and constrained upper routes. Retain successful elements after inspection.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse fitting pews, lectern, organ/choir details, archive and practical lighting assets. Arrange objects for actual worship/parish activity; maintain clear processional routes.
- **Acceptance focus:** Tall volume, roof structure, loft/crypt stairs, headroom and sound/light separation. Bell-tower or crypt branches must satisfy mandatory-objective fairness before use.

## Existing outdoor construction references

### WLD-01 — Blackpine Logging Camp (`wld-01`)

- **Organizing principle:** A timber worksite with log arrival, processing, stacking/storage, maintenance and separate crew life.
- **Rough/blockout:** Audit accepted-looking assemblies against current source evidence: machinery feed/output, logway, service access, worklights, terrain and canonical van delivery. Preserve the existing circular-headsaw corrections.
- **Reuse/refine:** Retain authored mill, timber, camp and crew kits where supported and coherent. Use debris/wear to explain the work sequence and repairs.
- **Acceptance focus:** Full-scale operational clearance, support contacts, power and visibility, hunt/ritual integration, host/guest behavior and actual Windows performance remain individual gates.

### WLD-02 — Camp Hollow Creek (`wld-02`)

- **Organizing principle:** A campground whose pitches, amenities, pavilion, paths, dock and shore follow actual terrain and visitor/service use.
- **Rough/blockout:** Preserve native route-grade, fitted facilities, water and source-geometry work. Inspect current scene hashes/adapters, van elevation, shoreline access, boundaries and pitch/service connections.
- **Reuse/refine:** Retain original campsite/pavilion/dock assets and accepted physical arrangements. Evaluate the woodland-matched palette in actual game lighting rather than replacing it from stale previews.
- **Acceptance focus:** Runtime water/shore agreement, van support, generator/circuit behavior, permitted hiding, evidence/ritual routes and multiplayer. The dock encounter remains separate optional implementation, not proof of mission readiness.

## Terrain-led and remote cultural sites

### WLD-03 — Red Mesa Ancestral Caves (`wld-03`)

- **Organizing principle:** Geologically coherent cliff/cave terrain with deliberately authored historical occupation and a believable roadhead approach.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve terraces, cave layers, daylight openings, ledges, stairs/crossings and safe retreat. Inspect existing elevation reservations rather than flattening them into ground rooms.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse rocks, appropriate timber/occupation traces and restrained site infrastructure; author missing rock transitions/supports. Research the approved cultural context before inventing decoration.
- **Acceptance focus:** Continuous cave/terrain support, no roof leaks or false floors, usable headroom, boundary containment and fair mandatory objectives. Natural form must remain legible at player scale.

### WLD-04 — Widow's Peak Fire Lookout (`wld-04`)

- **Organizing principle:** A staffed lookout with a real access trail, supported tower/cabin, service stores and observation purpose.
- **Rough/blockout:** Engineer the tower foundation, repeated flights, landings, guardrails, cabin entry, remote van roadhead and service path. Make the lookout's height meaningful in sections and views.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse appropriate timber/metal structure, radio, desk, bunk and service assets; distinguish living tasks from observation equipment.
- **Acceptance focus:** Ascent/descent and headroom under pursuit, highest-view skyline, weather exposure and extraction. A single constrained tower route needs explicit gameplay fairness review.

### WLD-05 — Dead Man's Claim (`wld-05`)

- **Organizing principle:** A mine with a believable adit/shaft relationship, extraction/haul route, ventilation, drainage and surface support camp.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve rock cuts, tunnels and level changes, headworks, service/refuge areas and mandatory exploration routes. Separate decorative inaccessible workings from playable mine passages.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse measured mine/industrial, rail, timber, tool and camp candidates where suitable. Place supports and equipment according to excavation and work flow.
- **Acceptance focus:** Tunnel support/headroom, water containment, powered lighting, route recovery and bounded ghosts. No graph-only shaft connection or unsupported tunnel deck can pass.

### WLD-07 — Saint Bartholomew's Forgotten Cemetery (`wld-07`)

- **Organizing principle:** A burial ground with readable paths, plots, memorial hierarchy, maintenance access and associated structures.
- **Rough/blockout:** Plan public arrival, procession/service routes, terrain drainage, walls/gates, crypt/chapel access and meaningful grave clusters. Preserve distinct elevations and sightlines.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse headstones, boundary/landscape, benches and maintenance assets with deliberate placement rather than uniform grave scatter.
- **Acceptance focus:** Ground-supported graves/paths, clear low-light route silhouettes, crypt transitions, perimeter containment and respectfully authored narrative traces.

### WLD-07-MORTUARY — Cemetery & Vale Mortuary annex (`wld-07-mortuary`)

- **Organizing principle:** An additive mortuary with distinct public reception/viewing and private preparation, storage, staff and service access.
- **Rough/blockout:** Review its connection to the parent cemetery, discreet delivery path, circulation/privacy thresholds and any basement routes. Do not count it as a separate base mission.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse appropriate office, domestic, medical, sanitary and industrial fixtures after dimensional/support review. Missing specialist fittings need explicit purpose and source records.
- **Acceptance focus:** Parent/annex zone and circuit integration, shared hiding budget, ritual/extraction compatibility and no doubled mission allowances. Staff/public paths must actually differ.

### WLD-10 — Monastery of Ashen Bells (`wld-10`)

- **Organizing principle:** Communal religious life around worship, cloister, cells, meals, work and service functions.
- **Rough/blockout:** Distinguish cloister circulation, church/chapel volume, refectory/kitchen, dormitory/cells, bell access and supporting stores. Resolve sloped foundations and outdoor links.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable church, household, timber/masonry and workshop assets while maintaining the monastery's restrained material language.
- **Acceptance focus:** Daily-use relationships and layered construction are legible; bell routes, cloister roofs, stair voids and dark service connections remain physically sound.

### WLD-11 — Mourning Marsh (`wld-11`)

- **Organizing principle:** A wetland crossed by purposeful raised/dry routes and sparse occupied/service structures.
- **Rough/blockout:** Author water levels, dry islands, boardwalk foundations, crossings, turning/retreat spaces, visible boundaries and a genuinely supported van roadhead.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable boardwalk, vegetation, camp/service and decay assets. Vegetation belongs to terrain/water conditions and never grants accidental hiding sockets.
- **Acceptance focus:** Full-body support on narrow paths, water/shore agreement, night navigation, under/around boundary exploits and visual/audio cost through dense vegetation.

## Waterfront, transport and industry

### WLD-06 — Drowned Fisherman's Wharf (`wld-06`)

- **Organizing principle:** A working shore connection with landing, transfer, storage, repair and sheltered crew/service spaces.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve quay/shore level, piers, loading routes, edge guards, shed entrances, water limits and dry approach from the van.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse dock, boat, rope, storage and workshop assets at real scale. Gear clusters must leave practical cargo/walking space.
- **Acceptance focus:** Pier support, water boundaries, credible understructure, shoreline lighting, occlusion and no route-dependent fall/softlock introduced by dressing.

### WLD-13 — Greywater Cannery (`wld-13`)

- **Organizing principle:** A food-processing sequence from receiving through preparation/processing to packing, storage and dispatch, supported by hygiene and plant.
- **Rough/blockout:** Lay out loading access, process hall heights, material/personnel circulation, drains, staff sanitation and service/plant rooms. Review legacy shell partitions against that sequence.
- **Reuse/refine:** Inspect existing industrial meshes before authoring purpose-specific processing fittings. Conveyors/tables need coherent inputs, outputs and maintenance space.
- **Acceptance focus:** Distinct working stations and sanitation relationships, supported machinery, walkable aisles, industrial acoustics and measured dense-scene performance.

### WLD-14 — Morrow Point Lightstation (`wld-14`)

- **Organizing principle:** A navigational station with tower/lantern, keeper life, stores, power and weather-exposed service access.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve coastal foundation, tower flights/landings, lantern approach, keeper building links, cliff boundaries and van roadhead.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse domestic/service/coastal kits where fitting; inspect or author the characteristic lantern and tower components at their proper scale.
- **Acceptance focus:** Real vertical route, believable support, highest coastal horizon, salt/wind exposure patterns and safe gameplay use of constrained tower branches.

### WLD-15 — MV Saint Elara (`wld-15`)

- **Organizing principle:** The existing retired catcher/processor is permanently moored; working deck/processing, crew life, hold, engine/bilge and wheelhouse occupy distinct real decks.
- **Rough/blockout:** Retain quayside van logistics, review both gangways, ship-shaped sections, deck openings, independent vertical routes and hull/water separation. The ship does not sail.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable maritime, industrial and crew assets; develop missing hull/deck frames and process fittings instead of disguising a shore building with railings.
- **Acceptance focus:** Sea never fills the hold, deck support and low headroom are deliberate, two gangways work, and cabins/plant/bridge retain credible connections and host-authoritative floor selection.

### WLD-08 — Northwatch Research Station (`wld-08`)

- **Organizing principle:** A remote research workplace with arrival/logistics, cold-weather transition, laboratories/work, accommodation and dependable services.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve entry/boot or transition spaces appropriate to the approved setting, connected work/living/service blocks, stores, generator distribution and sheltered routes.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable office/lab, bunk, sanitary, storage and plant assets. Specialized instruments need a demonstrated functional place, not generic console scatter.
- **Acceptance focus:** Remote service logic, severe-weather exposure, clear modular connections, blackout recovery and meaningful difference from ordinary campus/classroom plans.

### WLD-09 — Blackwater Hydroelectric Dam (`wld-09`)

- **Organizing principle:** A hydraulic/civil structure with coherent upstream/downstream relationship, control, machinery, maintenance and access.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve water elevations, dam mass, intake/outlet representation, powerhouse, galleries, controlled routes, stairs and service road/van staging.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse appropriate electrical, industrial and structural assets; author unmistakable civil/machine features where generic boilers or boxes do not fit.
- **Acceptance focus:** Correct water containment, credible large-scale support, guarded elevation changes, service access, acoustic separation and renderer performance across long/tall spaces.

### WLD-12 — Gallows Pass Railway (`wld-12`)

- **Organizing principle:** A railway site whose alignment, grade, cuttings/tunnel/bridge and maintenance access explain every structure.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve existing terrace/lower-walkway/tunnel concerns, continuous track support, pedestrian crossing/retreat, roadhead and boundaries. Do not flatten the railway into a decorated courtyard.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse existing terrain/rail scene-package work and suitable transport/industrial assets only through verified host bindings. Mesh export alone proves no game renderer/navigation integration.
- **Acceptance focus:** Actual shared terrain/rail support, tunnel clearance, credible bridge/retaining contacts, multi-level AI routes and exact source/runtime revision agreement.

## Roadside, defensive and underground sites

### WLD-16 — Eight Pines Motor Lodge (`wld-16`)

- **Organizing principle:** A roadside lodging business with reception, vehicle court, repeated rentable units and backstage housekeeping/maintenance.
- **Rough/blockout:** Establish road arrival, parking/cargo clearances, guest entry/privacy, unit bathrooms, office, linen/store and service routes. Repeated rooms should arise from an actual unit module.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse domestic beds, sanitary, reception and sign assets; vary occupancy/story after the room module functions.
- **Acceptance focus:** Courtyard/room transitions, doors/windows, privacy, bathroom access, service circulation and repetition performance. Distinct guest traces cannot substitute for a coherent motel layout.

### WLD-17 — Battery Brackenhead (`wld-17`)

- **Organizing principle:** A defensive installation with observation/control, emplacement, protected stores, service access and crew accommodation.
- **Rough/blockout:** Resolve terrain siting, earth/concrete structure, sightlines, protected links, ammunition/service movement and viable exits according to the approved historical concept.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse suitable industrial, bunk, concrete/metal and storage assets; build missing defining emplacement/defensive architecture explicitly.
- **Acceptance focus:** Thick-wall openings, bunker ceilings, coastal boundaries, narrow passages and exterior exposure. Weathering follows exposed versus protected construction.

### WLD-18 — Site Cinder-6 (`wld-18`)

- **Organizing principle:** The existing inert shaft/headhouse concept has annular service floors, controlled access, plant, records and crew support; retain its non-operational presentation.
- **Rough/blockout:** Engineer shaft liner/bottom, ring floors/guards, headhouse, escape stair, hillside exit, ventilation and sump routes. Audit vertical adjacency in sections before dressing consoles.
- **Reuse/refine:** Reuse inert racks, controls, office/bunk and plant assets where true dimensions fit; author missing curved shaft/ring/door modules. Avoid fake operational weapon behavior.
- **Acceptance focus:** Continuous independent escape, shaft containment, guard collision, actual floor selection, electrical distribution and no apparent walkable floor over an open void.

## Completion decisions

Each package advances through the same staged method with site-specific evidence. Maintain a visible distinction between retained work, newly rebuilt work, missing host integration and physical acceptance. The annex advances with its cemetery dependencies; Castle advances with its HQ contracts. Generalizing a fix across a shared asset family requires fresh fit review in every consuming map.
