# Monastery of Ashen Bells roof flashings

Three bell-tower abutments and four cloister gable joins now have folded flashing fitted to the measured slate and receiving faces. At the shallow gable shoulders, the flashing laps onto adjacent slate. The west tower gutter falls south and overlaps the refectory valley through a short descending bridge.

Software and OpenGL both passed 122 apron spans, 114 isolated flashing probes, and four bridge probes. The original 13 working doors, 84 route replays, and three walk-in refuges passed again. A live four-player session repeated the route from the van to the memorial chamber. All 574 retained collision records stayed byte-for-byte intact.

The archive–vestry seam and exposed eaves remain open. Supported services, furnishing, and named packaged mission acceptance also remain open.
