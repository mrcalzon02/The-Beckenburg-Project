# Ravenshade School — revised measured rough

1 November 2026. S02 is in progress. This is a new architectural design study, not the dispatched school, an accepted blockout or a statement of building-code compliance.

The rough authority is `tools/build_map_rebuild_roughs.py`. Its derived `rough-layout.json`, floor-plan SVG, stair-section SVG and volume OBJ describe 40 bounded architectural spaces mapped deliberately to the existing 15 gameplay owners. The PNG is a labeled planning diagram, not a runtime capture. Parent gameplay owners may contain several rooms; their old broad rectangles are not copied as invisible dividing walls.

The main teaching corridor is 3 m wide. Toilets, staff facilities and stairs occupy independently bounded rooms beside it. Each upper washroom is 7 × 5 m with a screened entrance reservation, three full-scale WC stalls and a basin zone. Neither is a corridor shortcut. A 2.5 × 3 m visitor WC opens from the public vestibule; staff sanitary access comes from the staff room. Nurse consultation/rest is private. Two changing groups each receive a 4 × 3 m bench/locker room and a 2 × 3 m washing room beside the separate PE hall. Individual fixtures, privacy screens, PE storage and physical door approaches still require blockout decisions.

General classrooms are 10 × 10 m with separate stores. Twenty-four original 1.1 × 0.65 m desks fit in two three-column groups over four rows. The proposed 0.9 m central aisle, side circulation and 1.8 m row pitch use actual desk/chair source bounds, not miniaturized furniture. Source hashes, pivots and GLB bounds are recorded in `rough-asset-bounds.json`. Final collider, chair-pullout, board sightline, door-swing and six-player movement proof remains open. The science room has separate preparation/storage; its specialist fit-out remains to be authored.

Dining, public gym access and food service have distinct paths. Food receiving, wash-up and refuse leave through the east service side. A visitor or pupil can reach the PE hall through dining's public connector without crossing the kitchen or nurse room. The 12 × 16 m multipurpose hall stays 7.2 m high with no phantom upper floor; it is not represented as a regulation basketball court. Upper assembly has its own 4.5 m clear height. Roof and bearing design remain structural work.

The main stair reserves a 3.6 m rise, 5.76 m run, 1.5 m clear width, twenty risers and at least 1.5 m end landings. Its full-run upper-deck opening is explicit. A separate service stair reserves a 3 m rise, 4.8 m run and eighteen risers; moving its lower end to Z13.5 leaves a real 1.5 m bottom landing. The lower plant footprint is reduced to X23–30/Z12–30 instead of an indiscriminate whole-school basement. That footprint requires a saved editor terrain opening; none is claimed authored for School yet. Basement and upper stairs use separate footprints.

Three 4.4 m ritual clear-area reservations occupy upper assembly, the cellar maintenance store and the exterior east side. The outside reservation was moved clear of the receiving wing; the source validator checks its building overlap. Hiding candidates, circuit/sound ownership, actual route lengths and hunt fairness remain later physical work. Canonical van position/yaw/cargo-to-entry intent is retained as a reservation, not replaced with another vehicle.

The LOC/NPS Public School No. 4 report was reopened: its description distinguishes phased corridors, sanitary facilities, nurse accommodation, gym and assembly additions. It informs the functional separation, not these game dimensions. No measured historical plan was copied. See `ARCHITECTURAL_REFERENCES.md`.

Outstanding S02 decisions include the secondary upper-floor exit, detailed sanitary/privacy and PE-equipment storage, structural support under the assembly hall and full terrain/parcel composition.
