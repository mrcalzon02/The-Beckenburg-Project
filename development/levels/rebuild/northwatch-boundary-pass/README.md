# Northwatch exterior boundary and roadhead

Northwatch now has a continuous steel windbreak on surveyed foundations and a closed vehicle gate behind the staged van. The service road continues into the exterior valley. The new terrain matches all 1,004 original rim edges and leaves both interior terrain openings intact. The work keeps all 426 original native collision parts, including the stair flights, slabs, and existing approach geometry.

Software and RTX 5070 OpenGL replayed all 20 links and three longer journeys in both directions and postures. The van-to-laboratory journey begins on the cargo deck and descends to the original tunnel. The survey records actual intermediate tread elevations at three distinct positions. Across the boundary, 651 standing eye lines and 1,172 supported crossing attempts stopped at visible construction. The same run checked standing, crouched, and jump-height positions on the barrier.

The terrain and perimeter are ready for the next Northwatch fit. Operating building doors, the full room and acoustic graph, utilities, safe zones, case actions, and named dispatch remain open.
