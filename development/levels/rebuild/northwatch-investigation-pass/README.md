The [ordinary game archive check](checkpoint.json) uses the same four native payloads and manifest as the [named station stage](../northwatch-named-pass/checkpoint.json). Replay with `python tools/verify_northwatch_named.py --investigation`. The driver compares the five archive entries against the named checkpoint and places the live result in `build/northwatch-named-review/investigation.tsv`.

One investigator walks from the van through the radio and archive doors. The archive witness receives the actual EMF observation and the host accepts the ward there. After the host refuses banishment in that room, the investigator steps through the array and wind-shelter doors, then enters the dormitory from the array yard. Each of the five closed leaves stops movement and the witness receives its open state.

The dormitory stair descends to the heated tunnel at −2.5 m. The next flight rises to the ground-floor laboratory with a supported exit. The laboratory banishment installation accepts the warded case and the host reports the settled result to all three peers. The paths follow the actual station layout in the [furnished export](../northwatch-furnishing-pass/checkpoint.json).

All three furnished refuges protect the investigator at two depths, from just inside the entry to the rear. Each is entered standing and crouched, and the player can conceal, reveal and walk out. Outside the enclosure the protection clears. The [prior measured checks](../northwatch-refuge-pass/checkpoint.json) cover protection across the fitted floor.
