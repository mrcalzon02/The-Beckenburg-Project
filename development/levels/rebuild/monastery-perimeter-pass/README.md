# Monastery of Ashen Bells estate perimeter

The new estate wall follows the western bank, northern shoulder, and eastern edge above the ravine. Its stone footings bear below the surveyed ground and pitched coping follows the wall. On the south approach, the fixed timber carriage gate stands between stone piers in an eight-metre bay beyond the staged van. Its roadway continues down the exterior slope.

The exterior terrain joins 1,137 vertices along the saved ground edge. The original terrain, interior cuts and 1,634 collision records are retained. The build reimports the exported render mesh and checks every new collision component against it.

The camera and movement tests approach the barrier from supported ground. Standing and crouched sweeps, an actual running jump and spectral movement use the game collision and room authority. The old road edge is the negative control.
