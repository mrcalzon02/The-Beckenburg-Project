# Monastery of Ashen Bells west-range power

The gatehouse and dormitory now have their own switched branches. The west and south cloister aisles share one switch because they share a gameplay room owner. Ten fixtures mount to the existing ceilings. The unswitched feeder follows the measured wall steps and uses sealed service sleeves at the dormitory and gatehouse.

Software and OpenGL verified all 28 installed lamps, 13 working doors and 84 existing route replays. In a live four-player case, an investigator used the west cloister switch without interrupting the dormitory or gatehouse. The dormitory switch also left the gatehouse powered. All three controls were reached through the existing floors and working doors.

The bell gallery and lower crypt circuits, outlying roof edges, furnishing, and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
