# Drowned Fisherman's Wharf walk-in refuges

The two cottages and net shed each have an installed walk-in cupboard. Every refuge bears on the existing room slab and clears the door swings, required approaches and assigned work areas.

Protection starts at each physical entrance and reaches the usable interior, including both rear sides and standing head height. Software and RTX 5070 verified 69 usable floor samples per refuge, standing and crouched access, and the zero-cover setting. With maximum cover enabled, all three players entered their own refuge, hid, revealed themselves and walked out in a staged TCP case.
