# Monastery of Ashen Bells northern slope drainage

The west interceptor runs along the hillside below the dormitory and archive. It receives the dormitory-north and north-range-west roof sumps and turns through the open ground beyond the archive. The east interceptor receives the vestry-side sump and follows the gentler ground east of the north range before turning into the lower northern soil. Each of the three sumps has a founded overflow sill fitted to its existing gravel bed and receiving channel.

| Graded interceptor | Measured fall |
| --- | ---: |
| Western hillside | 0.572 m |
| East of the vestry and north range | 0.067 m |

The terrain survey preserves 34 original footing contact elevations. The roof pipes, crypt, occupied foundations and doors remain in place. Structural views show both routes and their masonry details. The named game verifies the western and eastern bed samples against the same collision surface used for the rest of the Monastery. The earlier public, service and herb-court drainage is included in the same review.

The compacted GLB retains the exact live mesh data, transforms, materials and texture bindings from the previous scene, apart from the replaced terrain and three overflow rims. The editable Blender source retains the installed geometry. The revised export measures 8.81 MB, down from 13.50 MB.

The site remains in named review. Exterior vegetation and material finishing, loose objects, authored encounters, full investigation acceptance and the installer remain open. The OpenGL review archive was built with the normal Maven lifecycle using a temporary output directory and package-only mode. The generic atmosphere test still refers to a class outside its test sources.
