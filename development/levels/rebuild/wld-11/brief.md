## Place and normal use

A small settlement straddles a main raised boardwalk and a parallel service boardwalk. Homes, chapel and warehouse are reachable on dry supported decks; flooded terrain is a visible boundary.

Two stilt households and shared settlement work/worship, connected entirely through supported dry routes.

Proposed design program for normal former use; verify against measured assets and final plans. These are not historical facts, current NPC counts or changes to the six-player contract.
