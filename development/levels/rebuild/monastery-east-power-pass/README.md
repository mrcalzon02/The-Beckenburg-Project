# Monastery of Ashen Bells east-range power

The commissioned main supply now feeds four ceiling fixtures in the refectory and four wall fixtures in the east cloister. Each branch has its own reachable switch. The cloister feed branches upstream of the refectory switch. Its conduit runs on existing walls and ceiling, with two sealed masonry sleeves clear of the door openings.

Software and OpenGL verified all twelve new and retained light loads, 13 fitted door sweeps and 84 original route replays. In a four-player session the existing source and breaker passed the ghost trip, generator restart, reconnect and reset checks. The investigator then used both new switches after walking through the receiving and dining-hall doors. Turning off the refectory left the east cloister lit.

Remaining room distribution, outlying eaves, furnishing and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
