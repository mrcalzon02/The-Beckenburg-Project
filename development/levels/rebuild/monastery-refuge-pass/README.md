# Monastery of Ashen Bells walk-in refuges

The archive, dormitory, and lower memorial chamber each have a founded walk-in cupboard placed against an existing wall bay. Hunters enter through the open face and gain protection throughout the 1.58 m wide, 1.14 m deep, 2.02 m high interior. Protection ends at the physical entrance on exit. The normal hide action still controls concealment. At 0% cover all three cupboards remain walkable, and the sanctuary cue and effects are disabled.

Software and OpenGL checked 2,475 samples through the full envelopes and 60 samples by the interior corners. Both renderers preserved all 13 fitted doors, the 84 original route replays, and 12 cargo-to-refuge and return replays in standing and crouched postures. Live four-player sessions verified local movement, protection, hiding and exit in all three refuges at 100% and 0%. The cargo-to-refuge route replays use the actual van and existing cloister doors. The memorial chamber approach uses the existing catacomb steps.

This is a staged Monastery revision. Handrails, roof drainage, utilities, furnishing, and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
