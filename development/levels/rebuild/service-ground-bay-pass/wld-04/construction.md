# Lookout geographic terraces and stair bays

The station uses three saved geographic ground datums: arrival Y 0, middle bench Y 3 and upper clearing Y 5.65. Existing building floors and supported door porches stand at Y 6. Four separated hillside bays form two real ascent routes: L03 (0→3), L04 (3→5.65), L10 (0→3) and L09 (3→6, ending on the store porch). L06 shares L04, and L11 shares L09; no diagonal extra stair is created for either alternate link.

The hut east doorway and ruin west doorway receive level porches and two-riser entries from the clearing. The store south face is reconstructed around a normal indexed 1.8 m framed door, reached across its level porch. Whole hut, ruin and store floors replace the obsolete route-shaped cuts. Source-library props, pivots, UVs and raw source buffers are preserved.

The tower retains one founded braced frame with a Y 6 base, repeated switchbacks and supported landings at 8.25, 10.5 and 12.75, reaching the Y 15 cab and observation deck. Its south cab doorway meets the level access gallery, and its east doorway meets the level deck.

Saved terrain changes are geographic fields rather than navigation ribbons. The original height image and saved holes remain protected. Guidance lines in the architectural drawing and flow chart are explanatory overlays; they are never physical paths, collision, ground pads, terrain carving instructions or wall openings.
