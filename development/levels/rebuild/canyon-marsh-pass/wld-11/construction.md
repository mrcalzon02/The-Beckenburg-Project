# Mourning Marsh levee and piled settlement

The original van reaches a dry raised roadhead at 0 m. A complete founded low landing and a guarded, seven-riser shore stair reach the +1.20 m settlement deck. The main and service boardwalks, cross-decks and ferry apron are one continuous timber floor carried by foundation piles and ties. Seven fitted doorways join the homes, chapel, warehouse, shack and pump room to those full deck surfaces.

Twenty physical guide strips, the earlier diagonal stair and the fragmented exterior deck pieces were removed. All 1,517 seed terrain controls reopened. The standing water seen in the native review is a visible surface above the saved bed. Runtime water, complete containment, functional pump and circuits, enclosure finishes and room-capacity review remain open.
