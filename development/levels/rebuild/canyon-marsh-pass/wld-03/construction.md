# Red Mesa Ancestral Caves terraces and stair bays

The expedition roadhead, intermediate ledge and upper occupation terrace use the published editor landform at 0, +4 and +8 m. The shelter roof is continuous and an actual north doorway opens to the foot of an outdoor stair. Two separate retaining bays each rise 4 m over two twelve-riser flights. The middle landings are 1.82 m long. The original gallery flight still runs inside the building at its measured well. Its upper floor is one plate with guarded edges.

The lower cave has a level south threshold and a founded forecourt under the records alcove. Four piers connect the inner edge of the court foundation to the alcove floor. The return path passes through the lower cave, uses that real threshold and turns along the lower landform to the west shelf. The upper chamber crossing is a 3.30 m wide deck on masonry piers with a framed end door independent of the lower passage.
