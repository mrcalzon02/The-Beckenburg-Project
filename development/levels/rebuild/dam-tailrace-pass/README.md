# Blackwater Dam downstream channel

The east bank now has a 29.59 m reinforced channel seated on the surveyed trough. A continuous invert and side walls join two cast terminal bays. Both steel sluices are closed and retain the isolated 0.36 m water depth. The stage uses the exact saved bank, east hall foundation, catwalk and pier geometry from the previous checkpoint.

A water document drives the shared game renderer and submerged movement rule. Standing and crouched bodies can use the original catwalk; its guard stops direct crossings into the channel. The foundations, stairs, doorways and van routes passed their prior native checks on the new collision export.

The reach is currently isolated. The covered hydraulic connection, upstream reservoir, wider valley boundary, and named mission integration remain open.
