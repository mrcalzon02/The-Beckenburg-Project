# Blackwater Dam auxiliary power

The auxiliary generator and distribution board bear on the existing switchgear slab. The board has fixing rails against the north wall. Wall and ceiling raceways feed two separately switched circuits. Two fittings illuminate the switchgear and two illuminate the north side of the turbine hall. A sealed electrical sleeve joins the fittings across the intact partition. Investigators cross that partition through fitted door 880.

The staged scene reuses the previous room and door schedules verbatim. Sixteen van-to-control and return replays passed with both standing and crouched investigators. Both renderers confirmed that the four lamps go dark on source stop, breaker trip, and their own switch. A four-peer host session reproduced the trip and the walk from the van through three fitted doors in blackout. Restarting the generator kept the tripped breaker open across a client reconnect. The aimed breaker reset restored supply and the two room switches operated independently. The three walk-in refuges and fourteen operating leaves were checked again.

BW-10 covers the switchgear and BW-03 covers the north turbine bay at this stage. The generator’s combustion-air and exhaust provision, distribution to the rest of the Dam, supported water services, upstream works, and packaged acceptance remain open.
