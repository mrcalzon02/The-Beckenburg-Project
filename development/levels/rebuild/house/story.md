# Household story clusters

The current source preserves the existing Briar House public case history through the house canonical identity. Dining correspondence, cellar shelter records and the inherited mending tin are individually supported readable models. Interrupted tea and stored water form one household cluster; the child exercise book and wooden train occupy the actual desk; the saved frame and reading cup belong to the sitting room; the small bedside cup stays by the child bed.

Eight actual host-owned loose bodies replace automatic room-name scatter. Their mass, parent-local source binding, initial support and ordinary examination are exported in story-belongings.json. No new ghost clue, automatic evidence confirmation, persistent inventory or reward is introduced. Readable approach, pickup/ownership/throw and supported rest checks remain mandatory.

The optional location-specific encounter is not implemented yet. Its design, actual trigger/occlusion/state/reset/network behavior and visual inspection must be completed before full House acceptance. See FINAL_STORY_AND_EASTER_EGGS.md above this folder tree.
