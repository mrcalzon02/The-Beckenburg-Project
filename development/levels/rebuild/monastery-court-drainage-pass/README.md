# Monastery of Ashen Bells herb-court drainage

Eight existing roof downpipes feed grated limestone sumps. Each sump now has a real throat and a continuously falling clay pipe. The pipes run below the court's perimeter and cross aisles and end in two gravel infiltration galleries between the raised beds. The gallery ends receive the pipes above their gravel breaks. Two flush grates admit local surface runoff and provide access to the filter beds.

The courtyard grate assemblies and conductors share renderer and collision geometry. A bounded shoe span applies inside the two authored covers so normal movement can cross their narrow slots. The center aisle, outer swales, occupied foundations, and crypt are retained. The source survey checks the open pipe centerlines and the two inspection shafts. Named game checks replay the court and existing building routes, including standing and crouched travel across both grates.

The network uses infiltration. Verification covers the fitted geometry and its downhill connections. Groundwater and rainfall capacity remain outside this model. The northern slope, final materials, loose-object physics, authored encounters, installer and full named investigation remain open. The OpenGL archive used the existing package-only Maven invocation because the generic atmosphere test refers to a class outside its test sources.
