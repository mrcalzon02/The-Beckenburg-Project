# Beckenburg Penitentiary — cellhouse rough decisions

3 November 2026. S02 remains in progress. The revised rough contains 98 architectural spaces and 112 connections, mapped to the fifteen stable gameplay owners. Source authority is `tools/build_map_rebuild_roughs.py`; JSON, plans, sections and volume OBJ are generated review artifacts. This has not replaced the dispatched generic Prison geometry.

Each parallel wing now has eight independently entered single cells on each of two floors. The 32 ordinary cells and two isolation cells support the proposed 34-person normal-use capacity. A cell reserves 4 × 3.5 m externally, with wall allowance, a fixed 2.1 × 0.95 m bed footprint and full-scale WC/basin origins. The validator checks their native bounds and pivots against the room and bed reservation. The stock folding camp bed provides a compact-bed size reference; it is not selected as the final prison bed. A fixed steel derivative still needs authored geometry and inspection. No source master is resized or discarded by this plan.

Cell doors face the continuous 2 m gallery, rather than a gallery authored behind the cells. Each wing has two separated 1.5 m flights rising 3.4 m over 5.44 m, with twenty proposed risers, full-run deck voids, landing reservations and guards still to be constructed. Upper landings bridge to the cell-door side. The second flight gives an alternate return instead of one long trapped upper branch. Ground bypass aisles and dayhalls maintain circulation around the stair bays. Three shower reservations and a separate changing/bench area serve each wing.

The front sequence distinguishes public approach, identity reception, records, private search, holding, sally port and guard control. Visitors enter their booth side from the public approach. Prisoners enter a separate custody booth side from intake. Their common divider has no through-door. Public WC access remains outside custody; staff rest and staff sanitary rooms are distinct. Security gates require two-sided manual operation during power failure and lawful ghost events; drawing a gate is not proof of that behavior.

The rear passage distributes access to dining and medical facilities. Kitchen cooking/serving, food storage and wash-up/refuse are separately bounded. Deliveries arrive on the north service side. Medical reception, examination, recovery, clean supply and soiled utility are separate rooms, so patients do not travel through cooking or stores. Worship is reached from the observed custody passage without cutting through an occupied cell. The isolation range has two real cells, observation/control, clinical/linen service and washing/work space. Existing lore remains unchanged; imprisonment or illness is not used to predetermine a haunting.

Plant and laundry are below the yard at Y−3. The independent service stair rises 3 m over 4.8 m with separate landings. The complete X28–46/Z22–42 underground footprint requires saved editor terrain punching as well as an architectural stair opening. That cutout is reserved here, not yet authored. The yard must subsequently be shaped around the headhouse, foundations, paths and drainage under the terrain gate.

Three 4.4 m ritual clear-area reservations occupy the isolation washing/work room, medical utility and chapel. Their geometry is reserved before furnishing. The canonical van transform, ramp toe and approach remain in the staging data; arrival modeling must use the actual shared van. Outside rectangles describe site envelopes and service routes, not accepted terrain pads.
