# Monastery of Ashen Bells exterior drainage

The two gate downpipes still discharge to their gravel sumps. Each sump has a founded stone overflow sill leading into a shallow swale at the side of the forecourt. The western swale spreads into the lower western shoulder. The eastern one follows the outside of the terrace into the natural southern hollow. An independent swale intercepts the service court's uphill shoulder and discharges into the gully beside the refectory.

| Gravity section | Measured fall |
| --- | ---: |
| West gate overflow | 0.348 m |
| East gate overflow | 0.460 m |
| Service court interceptor | 0.653 m |

The three swales are part of the editable ground mesh and use the same triangles in the GLB and native collision archive. Old terrain chunks and the two overflow rim sections were replaced. The retained native construction records are byte-identical. Surveys preserve 94 grade points on the public approach, service court and garden. The ordinary named review checks the bed points against archive collision, replays existing door and stair travel, and checks safe-zone protection after the terrain change.

The site is still in named review. The northern slopes and the enclosed herb court need their own drainage and finishing review. Materials are at the construction review stage. Loose-object physics, authored encounters, the release installer and a full named investigation remain open. The OpenGL archive used Maven package-only mode because the generic atmosphere test refers to a class outside its test sources. The named OpenGL checks run directly against the archive.
