# Saint Bartholomew's Cemetery and Vale Mortuary boundaries

The masonry enclosure follows the burial lawn, chapel shoulder, roadhead and outer service yard. Both versions use the canonical cemetery gate behind the van. The annex also has its own fixed receiving gate, with the existing approach to the receiving door kept clear. Both road surfaces continue through their gates at the measured grade.

The external landform meets all 471 original cemetery rim edges and all 826 annex rim edges. The saved terrain and excavation remain the collision authority inside those rims. The record chamber, vault and access stairs retain their existing geometry. The only change to the original terrain render is the finish across the road throats.

Across the two versions, software and RTX 5070 checked 2,570 capsule contacts, 2,570 jump-height contacts, 1,155 optical masonry contacts and 2,468 supported crossing attempts. All 38 ordinary links and four complete stair chains pass in both directions. The staged boundary replay also verified the cargo ramp and court in both walking postures.

Operating building doors, full investigation room ownership and acoustics, working utilities and walk-in refuges remain open for both versions. Final landscape, drainage, sky composition and the mortuary roof still need their own reviews.
