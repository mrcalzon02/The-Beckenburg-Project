# Monastery of Ashen Bells stair handrails

Paired iron grips follow the existing bell tower and catacomb treads. The outer bell tower rail turns continuously around the existing intermediate landing. The 54 mm grips sit 0.96 m above the stair pitch line and bear on 58 surveyed stone mounts and end returns. The stage keeps all 417 preceding collision records byte for byte, including the treads, landings, wall faces, floor openings, stair guards, and refuge shells.

Software and OpenGL each checked 55 isolated rail collision samples, 408 standing and crouched tread samples across the central 0.60 m of the four flights, five supported standing views, and 12 direction and posture combinations on the three complete vertical links. All 84 original route replays, 13 door sweeps, and three full refuge volumes passed again. A four-player session repeated the actual walk from the van through the gates and down the existing stairs into the memorial chamber.

Roof junction and drainage, supported utilities and furnishings, and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
