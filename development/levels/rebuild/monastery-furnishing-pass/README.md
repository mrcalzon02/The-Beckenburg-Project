# Monastery of Ashen Bells ground-floor furnishing

The named Monastery review adds 23 fitted furnishings across the gatehouse, refectory, archive, dormitory and vestry. Four tabletop pieces and two readable records bear on the actual furniture tops. The existing building, door openings, stairs and power installation are the basis for the fit. The builder retains the prior collision records and generates matching render and collision triangles for the new components.

Aim at the Ashen Bells register in the archive or the ration sheet in the refectory to open the reading interface. The prompt and host both require a ray hit on the physical record. The named site is available with `-Dbeckenburg.review.monasteryNative=true` while ordinary mission dispatch remains locked.

The refuge review measures each physical threshold and tests standing and crouched entry and exit. It samples usable floor throughout all three cupboards, tests protection at head height and verifies that ghost movement is blocked across the threshold. Zero-cover difficulty disables the protection.

The captures show the supported fit from player height. The room surfaces and furnishings remain at the blockout refinement stage. Outlying drainage, loose-object physics, bespoke encounters, installer acceptance and a full named investigation are still open. The GPU archive used package-only Maven invocation because the repository's atmosphere test refers to a class outside the GPU test sources. The named OpenGL review runs directly against the built archive.
