# Monastery of Ashen Bells eave drainage

Five graded gutters collect runoff over the public gate and all four herb court approaches. Each falls 0.45% to a pair of lateral outlets. The ten downpipes are mounted to measured masonry and empty into founded gravel beds clear of the door approaches. The archive–vestry roof overlap now has a fitted step lap.

Software and OpenGL verified 132 gutter spans, ten open links from trough through notch, 50 bore rays, 40 inlet shoe samples, ten gravel beds, and 28 roof lap spans. All 13 operating doors, 84 route replays and three refuge shells passed again. A live four-player session walked from the van through the existing doors and stairs into the memorial chamber.

The outlying and tower roof edges, supported utilities, furnishings, and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
