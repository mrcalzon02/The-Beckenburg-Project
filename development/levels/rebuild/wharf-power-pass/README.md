# Wharf above-tide electrical supply

The service shed occupies a measured forecourt beside the shore road. Its slab is grounded across 713 samples and the framed roof bears on the installed posts and purlins. The generator and distribution board share the shelter, with a mounted lamp fed through the supported conduit. The shed approach leaves the van and cottage doors clear.

Both renderers passed the existing 18 links, the new service approach and the eight doors. The fitted lamp dropped to ambient light with the source stopped or the breaker tripped. In the four-peer staged game, a ghost trip cut power, the hunter restarted the generator without clearing the trip, a reconnect received both states, and the mounted breaker restored power.
