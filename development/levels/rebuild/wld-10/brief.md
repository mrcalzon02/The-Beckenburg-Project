# Monastery of Ashen Bells — first-principles function brief

**S01 IN_PROGRESS.** These are authored design requirements, not completed construction.

## Place and normal use

A compact enclosed monastery wraps a high garden, with a detached bell stair on the east and catacombs below the north range. Its service path and terraces distinguish it from the urban academy.

Twelve-person communal monastery with worship, meals, washing, work, archive and protected circulation.

Proposed design program for normal former use; verify against measured assets and final plans. These are not historical facts, current NPC counts or changes to the six-player contract.
