# Northwatch tunnel and yard electrical service

The generator now feeds both tunnel rooms, the instrument array and the service courtyard in addition to the eight occupied surface interiors. Protected conduit follows the shed wall and enters the basement through the actual retaining wall. The lower distribution is clipped to the existing deck and clear of the guarded stair openings. The array's switched return is buried beside the main. Five closed sleeves connect the assemblies across intact walls.

[Surface and lower service plan](tunnel-and-yard-service-plan.svg) / [Checkpoint evidence](checkpoint.json) / [Staged roster](roster.json)

The four new circuits feed six supported tunnel lamps and four outdoor lamps. Each reacts to its own switch, the existing source and the existing breaker. Software, OpenGL and the live host review exercise the connected routes, switches, normal doors and independent branch state.

Water service, furnishings, named case play and packaged acceptance remain open.
