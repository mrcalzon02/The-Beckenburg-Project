# Drowned Fisherman's Wharf operating doors

Four cottage frames take single leaves. The wider boathouse, net-shed and fish-house entrances take paired leaves. The leaves swing into their own rooms, clear of the outside walking surfaces. Native collision, the shared renderer and the acoustic query use the same fitted door poses.

The fifteen stable room owners include the two end-to-end stair bays and the canonical van. The graph uses eight actual door frames, ten measured open crossings and the real cargo lip. All eighteen routes passed with the doors open, in both directions and both postures.

Software and RTX 5070 verified open and closed movement, sight, objects and sound at all eight doors. In the staged four-peer TCP case the closed cottage leaf refused crossing, the hunter aimed and opened it, another player received the door state, and the hunter crossed into the cottage.
