# Monastery of Ashen Bells bell gallery and crypt power

The bell gallery, catacomb passage and memorial chamber have independent switches and eight new ceiling lights. Their risers are supported by the existing tower and vault faces. The two sealed deck crossings sit outside the original stair wells. The memorial feed branches before the passage switch and enters through a sealed sleeve beside the existing door.

Software and OpenGL verified all 36 installed fixtures, all 13 working doors, and 84 original route replays. A four-player case verified the generator, breaker, reconnect, and each room switch in sequence. The investigator used both bell flights and the existing catacomb steps to operate the upper and lower controls.

The current installation covers all twelve switched gameplay owners. Outlying drainage, furnishing, authored encounter and task content, and named packaged mission acceptance remain open.
