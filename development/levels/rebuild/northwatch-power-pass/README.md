# Northwatch shed power

The retained generator and water shed supplies the first operating circuit. The generator sits directly on the original slab. The breaker cabinet bears on a plinth and two rails fixed to the surveyed north wall. A casing-mounted terminal joins the source to the cabinet. Clipped conduit from the cabinet and switch junction serves the ceiling-mounted task light. The original radio door, lab door and guarded well retain their clearances.

[Measured shed layout](shed-plan.svg) / [Construction and runtime evidence](checkpoint.json) / [Staged roster](roster.json)

Software and OpenGL verify operating clearances and visible blackout in the exact native scene. The live TCP sequence trips the breaker, walks from the cargo van through the two aimed doors, stops and restarts the source while the breaker remains tripped, reconnects a client to the tripped state, then resets the breaker and operates the shed switch by aim.

Distribution to the other station buildings and tunnels, plumbed water service, case play and named dispatch remain open.
