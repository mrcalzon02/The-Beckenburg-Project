# Eight Pines Motor Lodge — needed models

Terrain and supported routes precede architecture and dressing. This is a worklist, not completed models.

- Deep floor/roof slabs with closed edges and downward-facing undersides
- Door/window frames and collision-aligned apertures
- Engineered stairs/landings/guards with actual deck openings
- Mounted fixtures, switches, breaker and supported cable/pipe runs
- Canonical van placement; no replacement van
- Fictional Eight Pines roadside sign and room number set
- Eight guest-room/bathroom door and fixture variants
- Exterior walkway, gutter, canopy and guard/step kit
- Reception key board, counter, phone and ledger
- Laundry machines, linen carts and plant-room fittings
- Parking markings, wheel stops, retaining bank, fence and hedge

Every named space has a room-by-room record in the adjacent JSON. Audit existing assets first; never substitute a wrongly scaled or wrong-purpose workshop model.

## Mandatory remote-site power

Placed generator, distribution board/main breaker and supported service wiring are required. Ghost breaker trips must affect the authored electrical loads through the normal authoritative circuit state. Generator running and breaker states are separate; oil/fire/battery sources retain their existing independent power rules. See ../power-plans/wld-16.json.
