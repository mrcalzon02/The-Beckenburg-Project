# Site Cinder-6 — needed models

Terrain and supported routes precede architecture and dressing. This is a worklist, not completed models.

- Deep floor/roof slabs with closed edges and downward-facing undersides
- Door/window frames and collision-aligned apertures
- Engineered stairs/landings/guards with actual deck openings
- Mounted fixtures, switches, breaker and supported cable/pipe runs
- Canonical van placement; no replacement van
- Empty cylindrical shaft liner, bottom and annular floor/guard kit
- Sealed headhouse and purpose-built escape stair/landing modules
- Inert consoles, equipment racks and records furniture
- Blast-door shell, ventilation ducts and cable-tray supports
- Sump pumps, grates, service lights and circuit labels
- Security fence, hillside exit, concrete pads and eroded banks

Every named space has a room-by-room record in the adjacent JSON. Audit existing assets first; never substitute a wrongly scaled or wrong-purpose workshop model.

## Mandatory remote-site power

Placed generator, distribution board/main breaker and supported service wiring are required. Ghost breaker trips must affect the authored electrical loads through the normal authoritative circuit state. Generator running and breaker states are separate; oil/fire/battery sources retain their existing independent power rules. See ../power-plans/wld-18.json.
