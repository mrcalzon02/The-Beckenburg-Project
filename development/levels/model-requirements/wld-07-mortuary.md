# Saint Bartholomew's Cemetery & Vale Mortuary — needed models

Terrain and supported routes precede architecture and dressing. This is a worklist, not completed models.

- Deep floor/roof slabs with closed edges and downward-facing undersides
- Door/window frames and collision-aligned apertures
- Engineered stairs/landings/guards with actual deck openings
- Mounted fixtures, switches, breaker and supported cable/pipe runs
- Canonical van placement; no replacement van
- Purpose-built mortuary preparation table, sink and cold-storage doors
- Viewing-room seating, curtains and discreet memorial furniture
- Reception desk, document cabinets, office furniture and supported paperwork
- Discreet service bay, trolley and screened approach
- Annex shell, roof/drainage, sealed partitions and mortuary fixtures

Every named space has a room-by-room record in the adjacent JSON. Audit existing assets first; never substitute a wrongly scaled or wrong-purpose workshop model.
