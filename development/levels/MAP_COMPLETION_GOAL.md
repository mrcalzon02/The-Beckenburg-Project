## Completion gate for each map

1. Measured architecture, terrain, storeys, real apertures and solid roof/floor joins.
2. Matching authored rendering, collision, room ownership, stairs, sockets, circuits,
   routes, ritual/hiding spaces, lore identity and canonical van staging.
3. Required reusable/unique assets, appropriate human scale, supported fixtures and
   small objects, physically clear circulation, restrained materials and detail.
4. Visual review at ordinary viewpoints and lights on/off; relevant runtime, route,
   interaction, evidence/camera, network/privacy and performance checks.
5. Discovered defects recorded; older-map audit obligations retained. Editable sources,
   packaged exports, actual captures and gallery uploaded and remote delivery verified.

Native human/multi-PC/hardware acceptance remains pending until real evidence
exists. A screenshot, metadata entry or passing graph is never sufficient alone.
Complete a reviewable current-map milestone before advancing to another location;
share checkpoints and continue the active goal rather than asking to restart it.
