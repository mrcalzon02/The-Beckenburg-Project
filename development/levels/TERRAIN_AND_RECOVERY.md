# Blackpine terrain, rooted planting and under-map recovery

The far camp edge at Z=1 is **1.78 m above the road at Z=72**, exactly one standing hunter body height. This is a roughly 2.5% overall grade towards the road. The road/van apron remains level at its canonical elevation. Buildings sit on level compacted foundation pads; adjoining mill/office/loft and mess/kitchen floors share their elevation. Doors, windows, stairs, voids, roof/fixture anchors, furniture, sockets, trunk/structure collision and exterior spawns are lifted by the same authored transformation.

`SiteTerrain` owns a baked **0.5 m grid with 20,698 vertices**. Walking interpolates the same diagonal triangles that are drawn and exported, rather than a visually similar second mathematical surface. Low raised shoulders, swales and pad transitions give the worn foot routes shallow relief. The original soil/forest diffuse is baked to one 1024×1024 map texture. There are no floating dirt/litter path stamps. `assets/runtime/level-textures/blackpine-terrain-diffuse.png` is a reproducible bake of the actual runtime material.

The arrival/shed route bends around the existing third refuge; the rear timber stack is in its own verge bay. Source vertex bounds of all logging piles, refuge shrubs and low scrub are checked against the worn-route width. Ordinary planting uses a larger route exclusion. The density pass keeps broad yard exposure and exactly three real hiding pockets.

Grass, flowers and soft foliage project their bases onto the actual terrain under each transformed vertex while retaining the global Y-up growth direction. They do not tilt their stems with the ground normal. Seeded botanical bends and crown twist remain independent of this root fit. Whole-instance culling bounds include the fitted roots. The fence rails follow the grade and have short matching physical barrier segments. The surrounding forest ground shares the same grade and joins the investigation terrain at its boundary.

`GroundRecovery` is a simulation safety net. Stationary LAN hunters and the host ghost are checked on the authoritative tick; solo hunters are checked in their frame update; loose objects are recovered in their physical update instead of deleted below the map. A pose below its relevant support is lifted to that surface. If a solid object blocks the spot, a nearby supported clear position is tried, then a validated room spawn. Room context preserves legitimate basements and upper floors; the van and stairs retain their own support. Loose-body velocity/attack state is reset after recovery. Clients cannot submit an arbitrary recovery teleport.

[Terrain and recovery checks](verification/blackpine-terrain.json) cover the exact overall rise, real shoulder relief, root fit, full shrub/log route bounds, uphill/downhill swept movement and actual hunter, ghost and loose-object recovery. The complete van flood and native-scene checks are linked from [Blackpine build notes](BLACKPINE_BUILD.md). This establishes the first height-field/texture/planting contract for later outdoor maps; those maps have not been converted or unlocked.

Current limits: shallow authored erosion is baked, not a live water/soil erosion simulation. The ordinary static log/structure meshes remain rigid on their authored pads. Native-window playthrough, complete hunts/ritual outcomes and target-hardware performance remain acceptance work.
