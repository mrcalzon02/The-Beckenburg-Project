# Reuse-first build order

User direction, 21 October 2026: finish the complete rough roster, then build one location at a time in the order that creates the most transferable assets and construction experience. Buildings should have normal believable dimensions; the **place** should feel large through exposed separation, negative space, sound distance and restricted refuge. Avoid enlarging every room or adding plentiful hiding cupboards to compensate for danger.

All 23 layout packets are complete. House, Farmhouse and School provide existing construction references. Their remaining acceptance work stays recorded; this plan does not reset them or claim they are finished releases.

## First Blackpine pass

**First construction checkpoint completed:** [Blackpine graybox and remaining gates](../BLACKPINE_BUILD.md). Crouch movement and sparse shrub refuge are in source, with physical and real-TCP checks. Continue refining this first site before starting build 2.

Use ordinary one-storey cabin/work-building proportions, not the larger rough room envelopes as mandatory dimensions: candidate cabin 6×8 m, shed 6×8 m, washhouse 6×6 m, mess 8×10 m and mill 12×18 m. Keep a large exposed timber yard and meaningful distances between shelter. These are authored gameplay candidates requiring first-person scale review, not externally measured historic dimensions.

Construct one connected site, preserve the standard van dock, validate routes and collision, then render the actual scene before expanding to the next location. Reusable shrubs must have stable host-owned placement and concealment rules across detail settings. Crouching lowers viewpoint and movement speed; it must not teleport, move feet through floors, bypass walls, or grant concealment without real cover. Sparse shrubs are contestable hiding, not safe extraction zones.

New architecture, outdoor refuge and posture each need their own targeted checks, visual evidence and scoped commit. No later map begins construction until the first map's state and next unfinished gate are recorded.
