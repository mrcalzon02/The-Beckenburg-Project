- Loop: entry → great → map → court → entry
- Loop: court → armory → workshop → infirmary → north → court

Every listed loop is an authored graph cycle, not a verified physical pursuit route. Cyan lines require later wall openings, surface support and clearance review.

## Systems and environmental identity

Facility districts have their own circuits, ventilation and acoustic transitions. Chapel, medical, workshop and relic storage remain different functions. Castle restoration may change decoration and service availability, never silently erase the only route or unlock a live hunt in the refuge.

## Campaign use

Assignment office holds regional dossiers; archive records discoveries; armory owns gear; infirmary supports recovery; lodging supports future hired allies; workshop/research/restoration support later services. Research and companion behavior, purchases, persistence and hiring remain separate unimplemented systems.

## Refinement risks / next pass
