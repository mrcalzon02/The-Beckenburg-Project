# Monastery of Ashen Bells

**WLD-10 · Highland Frontier · Medium-large / remote cloister · rough layout v1**

A compact enclosed monastery wraps a high garden, with a detached bell stair on the east and catacombs below the north range. Its service path and terraces distinguish it from the urban academy.

Cloister and garden are the main surface loop; service terrace links refectory to tower base. Catacombs have two independent descents. Bell chamber is a short observation branch with no required rite above a single stair.

- Loop: south → west → north → east → south
- Loop: north → archive → vestry → north

## Systems and environmental identity

Windward bell tower, still garden and stone underground have contrasting acoustics. Bell events require a physical bell/striker and lawful cause; the ledger is narrative, not a guaranteed supernatural reading.

## Campaign use

Silence, missing community records and an impossible toll ledger can connect to cemetery parish gaps. Monastic practice itself is not evidence of evil.

## Refinement risks / next pass

- Inspect monastery and bell-frame structural references before detailed shell work.
- Preserve the open cloister void; never cap the garden with an inferred roof.
- Engineer tower flights and two catacomb entries; all core rituals remain below tower level.
