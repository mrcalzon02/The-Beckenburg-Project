# Blackpine Logging Camp / Emergence and survival thread

Derived from `WorldLore.java`, the same history read in the Castle Common Archive. [Shared canon](../../../../docs/LORE_BIBLE.md).

- **Emergence Day, late 2022:** The camp was already closed when the supernatural returned in late 2022.
- **Survival and return:** Displaced survivors used one logging cabin in 2025; a caretaker later recorded their traces.
- **Environmental trace:** Pre-Emergence payroll discrepancy, later shelter journal and maintenance sheet are separate layers.

Layer pre-2022 use, the catastrophe, and later survival/reclamation. Records are attributed testimony, not current ghost identity or automatic mission evidence. Planned sites are story reservations, not completed expeditions or runtime content.
