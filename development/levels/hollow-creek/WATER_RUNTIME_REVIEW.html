Hollow Creek: water in the actual game

Four fixed, physically accessible cameras, looking toward the water. Each latest clip contains 72 fresh OpenGL game frames over six simulation seconds. Tier II flashlight, SOFT PSX preset; no native-render substitutes, added HUD or post-capture relighting.

Two independently scrolling pixelated layers, real wave triangles, slow shore run-up and broken crest foam, depth transparency over a new optical basin, guarded refraction, tinted Fresnel, actual inner-hull exclusion, and depth-aware distant blur fading to the camera’s sky. Upstream principles: MIT PSX Style Water Surface; its separately attributed demo texture is excluded.

The boat has three fitted seats, supported stowed oars, original weathered paint/wood/metal textures, and audited directional source-normal shading. The GPU atlas upload now preserves resolution and the correct V convention. Earlier broken captures remain below as defect evidence.

Work in progress: these are scripted actual game-renderer views. They do not establish mission-selector, full-round, ghost-specific breaker, host/LAN, human art or performance acceptance. Playback is encoded at 12 fps; it is not a real-time frame-rate benchmark. Creek-specific current, interaction ripples, weather presets, underwater transitions and true scene reflections remain later water iterations.
