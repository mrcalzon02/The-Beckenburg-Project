# Reproducible surroundings and physical light workflow

The standalone repository is https://github.com/mrcalzon02/The-Beckenburg-Project. All metre coordinates below use the existing game contract. Build changes, verify them, capture actual game images, preserve a scoped commit, fetch/merge concurrent work normally and push main. The old HB-TTRPG-tools checkout is reference only.

## Enclosure source

LevelEnclosure derives external edges from the union of outdoor support rectangles, including stepped corners. Each visible fence socket has a matching oriented collider at the same plane. Three reusable settings are active: suburban at Briar House, woodland at Hollowmere Farm and urban at Ravenshade School. Enclosed neighbour parcels reserve buildings from play space. Facade setbacks and the terminus position are derived from the shared road and checked against actual building volumes, fence planes and clear access lanes. Trees are placed beyond playable boundaries. Do not position a backdrop simply by eye against a screenshot.

The existing castle hub now uses CastleSurroundings: forest, continuous rocky mountain ridges, a road curving into the distance and a shut gate on its existing physical perimeter. Its playable architecture remains owned by CastleHubMap and CastleHubArchitecturalPlan. A mountain enclosure template is also available for future reuse. No new investigation-site category is unlocked.

VanArrivalTile and VanArrivalStreet own the reusable van/road module. Local -Z leads out of the rear cargo doors. Level docks rotate the module exactly once. For the current -pi/2 site yaw the road extends toward decreasing world X; the support margin follows that direction. Asphalt, footpaths, kerbs, road paint, vehicles and a canonical van anchor can be exported as an independent scene. Do not rebuild or mirror the road for each destination.

build_environment_details.py constructs original fence panels, trees, location plaques and inherited belongings. The lettering uses an original construction alphabet; no external font or image is baked into these assets. build_light_assets.py constructs the suspended bare bulb and six-arm candle chandelier, including real supports. build_worn_assets.py retains clean GLBs, native sources, previews, UVs and pivots byte-for-byte and writes separate aged textures. validate_worn_assets.py verifies that contract. Only original standalone files are dedicated CC0 in the public libraries; complete mixed-source game scenes retain their source licenses.

## Physical lighting

FixtureLighting describes actual world-space sources. Bare bulbs are omnidirectional; shaded bedside lamps have warm lower cones, weak upper openings and small fabric spill; six real candle positions produce warm omnidirectional chandelier light. Fluorescent housings use two separated sources and a downward beam. Active televisions emit white/cool light from the screen plane in its forward direction. Their original static loop and dynamic CRT image share the authoritative fixture state. Bedside lamps can be switched independently with exact E interaction. New washrooms have local physical switches and actual ceiling fittings; their master room circuit and breaker still apply. Candle flames are independent of electrical mains. The van has two actual cabin fittings on its own supply.

Light colour and attenuation depend on source-to-surface distance and direction. Architectural visibility prevents transmission through opaque walls, closed doors and floors. Door/window/hiding changes invalidate shadow caches. Surface samples are interpolated in perspective; large interior walls receive additional samples to retain directional patterns. Light-colour changes preserve geometry. View culling avoids calculating light for triangles wholly outside the camera frustum. This is a PSX software-rendered direct-light model, not a path-traced global illumination claim or a volumetric fog simulation.

Do not hide mounting mistakes with a larger ambient value. Determine the lowest real ceiling above each socket: the lower barn must attach under its loft deck; the upper loft and open tall barn bay have separate supports. Inspect from a traversable standing position. For close lamps and clocks, the legacy fixture command now uses the same exact visible mesh as E, avoiding proximity ambiguity.

## Review and source export

Run verify_levels.py with Java17 and the checksum-pinned original vehicle archive. PhysicalLightingTest checks real TV forward/back light, exact on/off use, room-door shadow changes and operating washroom switches. SurroundingClearanceTest checks neighbours against buildings, road and fence planes. LevelRenderAcceptanceTest renders every room with its electrical circuit on/off and checks actual pixels; candle rooms retain a separate electrical wall sconce. Existing geometry, swept routes, multiplayer, cupboard-density, held-device and original sound checks remain active.

LivedLocationCapture, EnclosureVisualCapture and PhysicalLightCapture write actual runtime images. A camera inside a wardrobe is not acceptable evidence; use the real operating point or verify standing clearance. Source export uses LevelSceneSource for HOUSE, FARMHOUSE, SCHOOL, ARRIVAL, ENCLOSURE_SUBURBAN, ENCLOSURE_WOODLAND, ENCLOSURE_URBAN, ENCLOSURE_MOUNTAIN and CASTLE_SURROUNDINGS. build_scene.py rebuilds their native Blender scenes from exact triangles, UVs and metadata. Embedded washer, TV, lamp and switch event anchors are empties; their meshes are already in the architecture and must not be duplicated. Exported light sources retain their kinds, colours, positions, beam axes, ranges and controller IDs. Reopen completed scenes with validate_level_scenes.py and verify native public libraries separately before publication.

Final delivery requires a real remote commit and live download verification, including hashes of GLBs and ZIPs. A successful push alone does not prove Pages deployment. Record actual results and any unverified limits in verification notes.
