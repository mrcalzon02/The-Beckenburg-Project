# Mandatory desquareify pass

User-directed, 23 October 2026. Apply **after rough spatial blocking and before
terrain construction is accepted**, then repeat after facilities or routes change.
This is a construction gate, not merely a prettier outline in a planning diagram.

1. Separate room/zone reservation rectangles from physical building footprints.
   Never bake a whole tentative room/campsite/yard rectangle into ground height,
   ground colour, a raised pad, or a broad square clearing.
2. Keep straight edges only where the actual object requires them: a measured
   building slab, constructed deck, road structure, or supported retaining wall.
   Remove oversized placeholder aprons and grade around the real structure.
3. Shape clearings around the actual facility clusters and use. Vary their radius,
   edge vegetation, drainage and height; feather their soil/material transitions.
   Worn paths cut into the surrounding terrain and turn naturally between destinations.
4. Bring playable edges toward useful space. Use lobes, narrowing access necks,
   bends, coves and landscape intrusions around the real routes and facilities.
   A loose rectangle with lightly perturbed corners does **not** pass.
5. Fit the terrain mesh itself, its surface treatment, and the visible physical
   containment to that shape. An irregular decorative wall around the old square
   ground or a hidden square pad does **not** pass.
6. Show the actual canonical van at its authored placement, including its raised
   deck and ramp, when reviewing arrival composition. A missing van or an envelope
   is not a complete arrival review. Never rebuild the vehicle to fit a map.
7. Inspect overhead and ordinary standing/crouched/photo views. Check ground
   relief, silhouette, path cross-sections, whole-site composition and highest views.
   Background vegetation/scenery stays beyond physical boundaries and receives
   real ground/root support; no floating tree islands or curtain across intended vistas.
8. Revalidate terrain support and grades, all route widths, facility footprints,
   ritual squares, van cargo access, ground contacts and barrier seams. Preserve
   failing evidence and log regressions in the shared defect ledger.

Record three independent results: **visual landscape fitness**, **native geometry
checks**, and **runtime/gameplay acceptance**. A graph, a renamed metadata field,
randomized corners, or one passing render cannot substitute for the others.

Older maps need individual audits. The original planning rectangles may remain in
editable planning documents; they must not dictate the final outdoor landforms.
Current Hollow Creek revisions are the first correction under this explicit gate,
not proof that every older map has passed.
