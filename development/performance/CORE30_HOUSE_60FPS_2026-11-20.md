The new default produces **15.0% lower solo-walking median frame time**, **14.7% lower TCP-walking median**, 12–13% more walking frames, and substantially better idle frame cadence. However **solo moving p95 is 2.4% worse and p99 7.0% worse**, while TCP moving p99 is 2.7% worse. No >100 ms interval appears in these specific measured windows; cold starts and first-peer-visibility are separate tests. Thus 60Hz is a real *median/cadence improvement*, **not yet a stutter fix**.

## Root-cause direction from archived raw frame CSV

The offscreen benchmark driver's `frames.csv` reports CPU renderer stages from SceneRenderer.FrameDiagnostics. Example **pair 1, solo walking**, individual p95:

| Component | 50Hz baseline | 60Hz candidate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Whole CPU renderer | 26.947 ms | 28.350 ms |
| Scene build | 1.250 ms | 1.290 ms |
| Geometry | 1.347 ms | 1.206 ms |
| Combined post-geometry effects | **23.376 ms** | **25.075 ms** |

The individual p95 values are **not additive**. This is enough to identify the combined post-render stage as a prominent walking-tail contributor, but not enough to tell whether the specific offender is sanctuary fog, character sprites, weather, PSX quantization, or other posteffects.
